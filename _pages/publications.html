Working Papers

Do Economic Incentives Increase Attendance at Blood Drives: Experimental Evidence from Sierra Leone
Job Market Paper.

Using a field experiment in Sierra Leone, this paper examines how financial, in-kind, and informational incentives affect attendance at community-level blood drives. The results suggest that direct financial compensation may be necessary to increase blood donations to adequate levels in low-income settings.
